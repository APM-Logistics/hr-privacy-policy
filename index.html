Privacy Policy — APM HR

Effective Date: April 28, 2025

APM Logistics respects the privacy of every employee and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the APM HR application, in full compliance with applicable standards including the Google Play Store and Apple App Store Review Guidelines.

User Scope

The APM HR application is an attendance and human resources management tool strictly designated for internal use by permanent and contract employees of APM Logistics. While the application is hosted on official app stores to facilitate seamless deployment across employee devices, it is not a public or general-consumer application. The intended end-users are internal personnel verified and authorized by APM Logistics.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Full name, official corporate email address, department, job role, and work schedule assignments (branch and shift details).
  • Attendance Data: Check-in and check-out timestamps, photo documentation of presence, and recorded geographic location at the time of submission.
  • Location Data: Precise device GPS coordinates captured during check-in/check-out to verify physical attendance within designated corporate premises.
  • Request Data: Details submitted through the application regarding leave requests, time off, and overtime.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and application version.
  • App Permissions: Camera access for capturing attendance photos and location services access for geofence attendance verification.
Account Creation & Management

User accounts cannot be self-registered. All user accounts are provisioned, maintained, and deactivated exclusively by the APM Logistics HR Administration team. Access credentials are provided solely to officially onboarded employees.

2. Use of Information

We use the collected information for the following business and employment purposes:

  • Recording employee attendance, tracking shifts, and calculating hours worked.
  • Verifying physical location and validating attendance records in accordance with company policy.
  • Processing and managing leave, permission, and overtime requests.
  • Administering payroll calculations, employee allowances, and related HR administrative functions.
  • Maintaining and enhancing application security, reliability, and functionality.
  • Providing technical assistance and internal user support.

3. Specific Policy on Face Data

In compliance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Section 5.1.1 and 5.1.2), this section details our specific policies regarding the collection, use, disclosure, sharing, retention, and deletion of facial information (face data):

  • Collection and Purpose of Use: The application captures a live facial photograph using the device camera exclusively at the moment of attendance recording (check-in and check-out). This face data is collected and processed solely to visually verify the employee's physical identity on-site, prevent fraudulent attendance ("buddy punching"), and maintain authentic corporate attendance records. We do not use face data for cross-app tracking, targeted marketing, behavioral profiling, or third-party advertising.
  • Sharing and Disclosure: We never sell, lease, or trade face data to any third parties. Access to face data is strictly restricted to authorized HR personnel and management of APM Logistics. Where face data is stored or processed by trusted third-party cloud infrastructure providers (such as secure cloud database and hosting providers), we ensure through legally binding data processing agreements that each third party guarantees the same or equal level of protection for face data as defined in this Privacy Policy.
  • Retention and Storage: Facial attendance photos are securely stored using industry-standard encryption (both in transit and at rest) on secure servers. Face data is retained only for the duration of the employee's active employment and for the minimum statutory period necessary for HR audit, dispute resolution, or compliance with applicable labor regulations. When an employee departs or when the retention period expires, the face data is permanently deleted or anonymized.
  • Consent, Revocation & Deletion Policy:
    • Mandatory Corporate Policy & Consent: The collection and verification of facial photos during attendance recording is a mandatory condition of employment and internal corporate policy at APM Logistics. Consequently, active employees cannot independently delete or request selective erasure of their historical attendance face data while actively employed, as these records serve as statutory legal and financial evidence for payroll disbursement, labor compliance, and operational audit integrity.
    • Revoking Camera Permissions: Employees retain control over their device permissions and may revoke camera access at any time through their device settings (Settings > APM HR > disable Camera). However, revoking this permission will prevent the employee from completing photo-verified attendance check-ins/check-outs, which may impact attendance validation under company employment regulations.
    • Data Deletion Upon Termination of Employment: Automatic deletion of face data occurs upon the termination or conclusion of the employment relationship. Once an employee is formally offboarded and all statutory retention requirements for financial and employment audit (typically up to 5 years under applicable labor and tax laws) have elapsed, all associated facial records are permanently purged and destroyed from company servers. Ex-employees may submit an inquiry regarding the status of their post-employment data lifecycle by contacting the HR Administrator at admin@apmlogistics.id.

4. General Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. Personal data is shared strictly under the following circumstances:

  • Authorized HR administrators and internal management of APM Logistics on a strict need-to-know basis.
  • Third-party service providers essential for application infrastructure (e.g., secure cloud hosting and mapping services).
  • Regulatory, judicial, or law enforcement bodies when mandated by applicable law or official legal process.
Third-Party Safeguards

All external service providers undergo rigorous security vetting and are bound by contractual confidentiality and data protection agreements requiring appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards.

5. Data Security

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. These measures include TLS encryption for data in transit, AES encryption for stored sensitive assets, strict role-based access control (RBAC), and regular infrastructure security reviews.

6. General Data Retention

Personal data is maintained for the duration of employment and retained thereafter only as required by applicable tax, corporate, or employment laws. Once data is no longer necessary for employment administration or legal compliance, it is securely destroyed or irreversibly anonymized.

7. User Rights

Subject to applicable regulations and company internal employment policies, employees possess the right to:

  • Access and review their attendance logs and personal records.
  • Request correction or updates to inaccurate administrative or profile information.
  • Revoke granted device permissions (such as camera and location access) at any time via device settings.
  • Inquire regarding the lifecycle and statutory retention periods of their records following offboarding.

Note: Mandatory compliance and attendance verification data (including facial attendance logs) cannot be altered or selectively deleted during active employment due to corporate audit and payroll verification requirements.

To exercise any of these rights or for questions, please contact our HR team via the contact details provided below.

8. Children's Privacy

The APM HR application is strictly an enterprise workplace tool and is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our operational procedures, legal mandates, or platform guidelines. The most current version will always remain accessible on this page. Significant modifications will be communicated through internal announcements or application release notes.

10. Platform Compliance

The APM HR application complies with both Apple App Store Review Guidelines (including specific clauses on App Privacy, Human Interface, and Face Data usage) and Google Play Store Developer Policies (Data Safety). As noted in the User Scope section, this software serves strictly internal corporate workflows and is not a public commercial application.
